About Us

At Marks Metal Signs, we work closely with our clients to design, fabricate, and install stainless steel address signs for multi-family housing communities.

The process starts with real-time design using our unique tool.

01

The Design Your Sign Tool

We developed the Design Your Sign tool so you can see what your sign looks like in real time. No more waiting to get a design proof back.

Just enter the numbers you’d like, select the type of sign, and your sign will appear. You can then make adjustments to see what the sign will look like with:

  • Different fonts
  • Siding types
  • Paint colors

As you experiment with your signs, your order estimate will update automatically. No more guessing at what the quote will be.

02

Overbuilt, High-Quality Address Signs

We fabricate our signs from 11 gauge 304 stainless steel rather than soft aluminum. The signs won’t rust, corrode, dent, or bend easily.

Each address sign comes with screws for mounting and spacers for irregular siding. We provide mounting templates with every order. No need to guess at measurements.

03

Bulk Sign Orders Welcome

We’re happy to design and quote multi-unit properties up to hundreds of units at a time. All of your unit numbers will match: no more random mix of fonts and sizes.

At Marks Metal Signs, we believe that no project is too big or too small. We ensure that we deliver high-quality, durable signs on time and within your budget.
