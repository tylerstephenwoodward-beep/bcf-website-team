Privacy

The short version: we only collect what you type into a quote form, and only to answer your quote. We never sell or share your information. The site has no ads, no tracking and no cookies.

What we collect

  • Quote and custom requests: your name, email, phone (if you add it), your design choices and any notes. We use them to reply with a price and to make your order, and for nothing else.
  • Nothing else. Browsing the site, using the design tool and trying fonts or finishes doesn't send us anything.

Photos of your wall

A photo you add in the design tool stays in your browser. It only draws the preview. It is never sent to us, never stored on a server, and is gone when you close the page.

If you scan the code to take the photo on your phone, the photo travels directly from your phone to the design page on your computer over an encrypted connection. Before sending, your phone shrinks the photo and removes its location data. The site never turns on your camera by itself. It only opens when you tap the button to take a photo, and your phone may ask your permission first.

Services that help run the site

Each of these services handles one small part of the site. Only Web3Forms and our email inbox see your quote details.

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it keeps short-lived logs of visits (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the service.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the site's fonts and code libraries. Your browser asks them for files, so they see your IP address, as any website would.
  • Web3Forms delivers quote requests to our email inbox (Gmail).
  • PeerJS introduces your phone to your computer when you use the phone-photo code. It never receives the photo.

Cookies and storage

We don't use cookies or tracking. After you send a quote request, a copy of your choices is kept in your browser tab so the next page can show you a summary. It is deleted when you close the tab.

Your choices

Email us to see, correct or delete anything you've sent us, and we will. The site is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
