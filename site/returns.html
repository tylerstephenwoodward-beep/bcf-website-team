Returns Policy

Effective April 1, 2026 · Last updated October 20, 2026 · Marks Metal Signs (Marks LLC)

Custom steel address signs are made to order and final sale. If there's a manufacturing defect or an error on our part, contact us right away and we'll make it right.

Overview

Thank you for choosing our custom metalworking services. We take pride in crafting high-quality, hand-forged home hardware and personalized steel address signs. Please review our returns policy carefully before placing your order.

Return eligibility

Hand-forged home hardware

  • Return window: stock (non-custom) items may be returned within 7 days of receipt.
  • Condition: items must be unused, in original condition, and include all packaging materials.
  • Return shipping: customers are responsible for return shipping costs.
  • Refund processing: refunds will be issued within 10 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

Custom steel address signs

  • Returns and exchanges: due to the personalized and unique nature of custom address signs, all sales are final. No returns or exchanges can be accepted once the sign has been fabricated.
  • Exceptions: in the event of manufacturing defects or errors on our part, please contact us immediately. We will work with you to resolve the issue.

How to start a return

  1. Contact us within the return window to request a return authorization.
  2. Pack the item securely in its original packaging.
  3. Ship the item to the address below.
  4. Include proof of purchase and your reason for the return.

Non-returnable items

  • Custom steel address signs
  • Custom ironwork (the listing will clearly note when a product is custom)
  • Items damaged by customer misuse or improper installation
  • Items returned after the 7-day window
